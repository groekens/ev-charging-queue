Setup Guide

How to find your Discord ID

Get @mentions when it's your turn to charge.

1

Click Settings (Gear Icon)

At the bottom left of Discord, next to your username, click the gear icon.

2

Enable Developer Mode in Advanced Settings

In the settings menu on the left, scroll down and click "Advanced", then toggle "Developer Mode" to ON (it will turn blue).

3

Copy Your User ID

Close settings, then click on your profile picture (bottom left) and select "Copy User ID".

Your Discord ID will look something like: 123456789012345678

⚠️ Important: Your Discord ID is a long number (usually 18 digits). If you copy something else, go back and make sure Developer Mode is enabled first!
4

Paste It in the App

Go back to the EV Charging Queue app and paste your Discord ID in the "Discord ID" field.

That's it! You'll now receive @mentions in Discord when it's your turn to charge. 🎉

💬 Why do we need this?
Your Discord ID allows the app to send you proper @mentions, which trigger push notifications on your phone. Without it, you'll just see your Xgram in messages (like "@ABC") but won't get notified.
